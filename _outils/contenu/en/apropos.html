About me

I am a master's student in political science (thesis track) at Université Laval, after completing a Bachelor of Laws at the same university.

Passionate about politics and history, I love to understand how states, institutions and citizens interact, here and elsewhere.

My path is shared between research and parliamentary life. I am a research assistant at the Chaire de leadership en enseignement des sciences sociales numériques (CLESSN) and at the Canada Research Chair in International Political Economy. I was also a page at the National Assembly of Québec and a student researcher at the Court of Appeal of Quebec.

Since 2023, I have been involved in the Quebec Youth Parliament, where I was notably Minister of Democratic Institutions and international delegate to the Conseil des Jeunes Valdôtains, in Italy.

Interests

  • Democratic institutions and parliamentary life
  • International relations and global governance
  • International political economy
  • Law and public policy
